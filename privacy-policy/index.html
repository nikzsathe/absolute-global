PERSONALLY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION AND OTHER INFORMATION

  1. When you use our Website, we collect, receive, and store your personal information and other information which is provided by you from time to time.

We use this information to do internal research on our users’ demographics, interests, and behavior. This information is compiled and analyzed on an aggregated basis.

We also use data collection devices such as “cookies” on certain pages of the Website to help analyze our web page flow, measure promotional effectiveness, and promote trust and safety. “Cookies” are small files placed on your hard drive that stores certain information that can be read back later. You are always free to decline our cookies if your browser permits, although in that case, you may not be able to use certain parts or features on the Website and you may be required to re-enter your password more frequently during a session.   6. Time period till which your data will be stored: We usually solely retain your data for the time necessary to comprehend our legitimate business functions and to befits the law.
  7. We do use your contact information to send you materials that may be of your interest.
